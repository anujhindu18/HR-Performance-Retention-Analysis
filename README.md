# HR Performance & Retention Analysis

## Project Overview
HR Performance & Retention Analysis is a data analytics project developed using **SQL Server and Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee attrition, workforce performance, job satisfaction, and retention trends.

The project focuses on identifying factors that influence employee turnover and understanding how job roles, departments, overtime, salary hikes, work-life balance, and employee satisfaction affect retention.

## Dataset
- Dataset downloaded from Kaggle.
- Contains employee-related information, including demographics, job roles, salary, education, satisfaction levels, and attrition.
- Used for HR analytics and workforce performance analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL Server (SSMS)
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Kaggle (Dataset Source)

## Key Analysis
The dashboard includes the following analysis:

- **Job Level Analysis:** Employee attrition across different job levels.
- **Job Involvement:** Relationship between job involvement and attrition.
- **Work-Life Balance:** Impact of work-life balance on employee retention.
- **Job Satisfaction:** Analysis of employee satisfaction levels.
- **Environment Satisfaction:** Relationship between workplace satisfaction and attrition.
- **Business Travel:** Attrition based on employee travel frequency.
- **Overtime Analysis:** Comparison of attrition among employees working overtime and those who do not.
- **Department Analysis:** Department-wise employee attrition.
- **Years in Current Role:** Relationship between time spent in the same role and employee turnover.
- **Distance from Home:** Analysis of attrition based on employee commuting distance.
- **Salary Hike Analysis:** Attrition across different salary hike percentage groups.
- **Education Analysis:** Employee attrition based on education levels.
- **Job Role Analysis:** Comparison of attrition across different job roles.

## Business Questions
1. What is the overall attrition rate?
2. Which departments are facing the highest attrition and why?
3. How does job role influence attrition rates?
4. Is there a correlation between distance from home and attrition rate?
5. Is overtime contributing to higher attrition?
6. How do job satisfaction and environment satisfaction affect employee retention?
7. Are employees with low work-life balance scores more likely to quit?
8. Are employees with frequent business travel more likely to leave?
9. Does the number of years in the same role correlate with the likelihood of leaving?
10. Are salary hikes influencing employee retention effectively?

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive Job Role, Job Level, and Gender filters.
- KPI cards for employee count and attrition metrics.
- Donut charts for business travel, overtime, and department analysis.
- Bar charts for education and salary hike analysis.
- Line charts for years in current role and distance from home.
- Treemap for job role-wise attrition analysis.

## Project Objective
To transform HR data into meaningful business insights that help organizations understand employee turnover, identify retention challenges, monitor workforce performance, and support data-driven HR decisions.

## Conclusion
This project provides an interactive view of employee attrition and retention patterns. It helps identify workforce trends and explore potential factors associated with employee turnover, supporting better HR planning and employee retention strategies.
